## MTR Schedule Manager 1.2.1

A network map release: more interchange styles, map icons and cleaner line bundles.

### 🗺️ Network map
- **11 interchange styles**, each with its own station marker. New: white rings, coloured rings with a bridge, squares, a colour-sector hub and a small "dumbbell on the lines"
- **Neater interchange dots**: always symmetric. They are laid out as a row, a 2+1 triangle, a 2×2 square or a honeycomb, with one dot per route (both directions of a route share a dot)
- **Map icons**: 18 categories (park, museum, station, airport…), 3 drawings each, on a round or square plate in any colour. The palette opens next to the tool. You can also upload your own picture (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, SVG)
- The selected line is highlighted
- "Align in a line" for selected stations
- A preview of what a rebuild from MTR will add, remove or rename
- **Cleaner bundles**: no zigzags or half-lane steps at stations, no gaps on the bends of thick lines, and merged lines shift together

### 🛠 Fixes
- The "Route types" counter now shows only the types selected on this server
- Network map settings are fully translated into DE / FR / PL / PT / CS (about 100 strings were still in English)
