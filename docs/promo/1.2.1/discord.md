## 🚆 MTR Schedule Manager **1.2.1** is out!

A smaller update, focused on making your network maps look great 🗺️

**🔀 11 interchange styles**
Bar, capsule, dumbbell, white rings, coloured rings, squares, a colour-sector hub and more. Every style now has its own look, and the interchange dots are always symmetric: a row, a triangle, a 2×2 square or a honeycomb. You get one dot per route.

**📍 Map icons**
Put parks, museums, stations, airports, stadiums and more on your map. There are 18 categories with 3 drawings each, on a round or square plate in any colour. Or upload your own picture.

**✨ Polish**
• Cleaner line bundles: no zigzags, no little steps at stations
• The selected line is highlighted
• "Align in a line" for stations
• A preview before rebuilding from MTR
• Network map settings fully translated 🇩🇪🇫🇷🇵🇱🇵🇹🇨🇿
